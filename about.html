About Ganesh Utsav 2026

The story behind the celebration and how your contribution helps.

🏛️

Our Celebration

Ganesh Utsav at IIT Mandi brings together students, faculty and staff for a vibrant multi-day festival of devotion, culture and community.

🌱

Meaningful Impact

Funds raised go toward ceremonial arrangements, cultural performances, decorations and community initiatives across the institute campus.

🤝

Transparent Giving

Every contribution is verified against official payment records by the organizing team before it is celebrated publicly on the leaderboard.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your IIT Mandi account.
  2. Choose your category — Student, Faculty, or Staff.
  3. Pay via the official UPI method (QR / UPI ID). We never ask for card, OTP or UPI PIN details.
  4. Submit the transaction reference for organizer verification.
  5. Get verified and appear on the public leaderboard (unless you choose to stay anonymous).
